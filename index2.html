Key Features

RaktSetu offers a comprehensive ecosystem for blood donation and distribution

🔍

Smart Blood Search

Find blood by type, location, and availability with our intelligent search system that provides alternatives when exact matches aren't available.

🤖

AI Assistant (Wily)

Navigate the platform with our chat and voice-enabled AI assistant that helps you find what you need quickly in emergencies.

🚨

Emergency SOS

One-tap emergency blood request feature that prioritizes your needs and alerts nearby donors and blood banks.

📊

Live Blood Tracking

Real-time visibility of blood stock across connected blood banks with intelligent suggestions for alternatives.

🚚

Secure Delivery

Track your blood delivery in real-time with specialized medboxes that maintain temperature and sterility.

Blockchain Verification

Transparent blood unit tracking from donor to recipient with immutable blockchain records ensuring safety and authenticity.

Who Can Use RaktSetu?

Our platform serves multiple stakeholders in the blood donation ecosystem

Patients

Search for blood, place requests, track deliveries, and access emergency SOS features when time is critical.

Donors

Register as a donor, track your eligibility, schedule donations, and see the impact of your contributions.

Blood Banks

Manage inventory, handle requests, verify blood units, and use AI-powered demand forecasting.

Hospitals

Request blood for patients, manage inventory, and track deliveries with real-time updates.

Delivery Partners

Manage delivery requests, provide real-time updates, and ensure safe transport with medbox monitoring.

Remote Users

Access blood services via SMS or USSD when internet connectivity is limited or unavailable.
